# Define or evaluate product vision

*Canonical skill id: `define-product-vision`*

Source of truth: [`SKILL.yaml`](SKILL.yaml) (schema `guild.skill-manifest/v1`).

## Goal

Establish or evaluate a product vision, outcome and priority against which requirements and hypotheses are judged.

## Applicable profiles

product-owner

## Inputs

- Business goals or stakeholder input
- Optional: a proposed hypothesis or opportunity to evaluate

## Outputs

- Vision/roadmap update or a prioritization decision

## Steps

- State the intended outcome in outcome terms, not solution terms.
- Check the proposal against the existing roadmap and scope.
- Decide whether to pursue, defer or reject, and record why.
