# Define requirements

*Canonical skill id: `define-requirements`*

Source of truth: [`SKILL.yaml`](SKILL.yaml) (schema `guild.skill-manifest/v1`).

## Goal

Turn an approved goal into actors, business rules, dependencies, edge cases and testable acceptance criteria.

## Applicable profiles

business-analyst

## Inputs

- Prioritized goal or hypothesis from the Product Manager

## Outputs

- Requirements artifact
- User stories with acceptance criteria

## Steps

- Identify the actors and business rules involved.
- Enumerate dependencies and edge cases.
- Write acceptance criteria specific enough to verify without interpretation.
- List open ambiguities instead of resolving them by assumption.
